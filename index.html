Common questions

What are the puppy plan prices?
Golden Retriever puppy plan from $3,500 AUD; Poodle puppy plan from $4,000 AUD; Siberian Husky puppy plan from $4,500 AUD. The $750 Butler Service Fee applies on top of every plan for concierge matching, breeder vetting, paperwork checks, and Brisbane-area delivery coordination.
What is included in the $750 Butler Service Fee?
Consultation, personalised breed matching within your chosen plan, vetting of breeders, health record verification, coordination of transport and delivery logistics, plus a welcome puppy kit.
Are your puppies health checked and vaccinated?
Absolutely. All puppies come with vet health certificate, age-appropriate vaccinations, microchip, and a 12-month genetic health guarantee from our partner breeders.
Where do you deliver?
Our concierge service is centred on Brisbane, Queensland. We coordinate handover across Greater Brisbane and nearby areas using approved, climate-appropriate pet transport partners.
How long does the matching process take?
Sourcing and preparation vary by litter and breed. Under our standard terms, delivery may take more than one hundred and eighty (180) days from the date your placement is confirmed in writing — see our highlighted terms below. We keep you updated throughout.

Terms & conditions — delivery timeframe

Delivery window. By engaging The Puppy Butler, you acknowledge and agree that completion of your puppy placement — including sourcing, veterinary and breeder checks, documentation, and physical delivery or handover to you in Brisbane, Queensland — may take more than one hundred and eighty (180) calendar days from the date we confirm your reservation or written agreement (whichever is earlier), unless a different period is expressly agreed in writing by both parties.

Estimates, not guarantees. Dates we discuss are planning estimates only. Breeding cycles, transport availability, regulatory requirements, and animal welfare considerations can affect timing. We will communicate material delays in good faith; the delivery timeframe may exceed 180 days depending on operating conditions.

Service area. Standard delivery coordination applies to addresses in Greater Brisbane and surrounding Queensland locations we confirm as serviceable. Enquiries outside this area may be declined or subject to separate written arrangements and additional fees.

Acceptance. Proceeding with a consultation, waitlist, or paid service fee constitutes your acceptance of these delivery terms. For a full legal agreement, request our complete terms of service before payment.
